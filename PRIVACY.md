# Privacy Policy for Expand

_Last updated: May 7, 2026_

Expand is a browser extension that lets you view diagrams, images, and tables in a full-screen lightbox with zoom, pan, and export.

## Summary

**Expand does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal information or user data.**

## What we collect

Nothing. Expand does not collect any data about you or your browsing.

- No personal information
- No usage analytics or telemetry
- No browsing history
- No page content
- No cookies, identifiers, or tracking of any kind

## What we send

Nothing. Expand does not send any data to us, to our servers, or to any third party. The extension has no backend and makes no network requests of its own.

## What we store locally

Expand uses your browser's local `storage` API only to remember your own extension preferences (such as toolbar and theme settings) on your device. This data:

- Stays on your device
- Is never transmitted anywhere
- Can be cleared at any time by removing the extension

## Permissions

Expand requests the minimum permissions needed to function:

- `activeTab` – to detect and display diagrams, images, and tables on the page you are currently viewing, only when you interact with the extension.
- `storage` – to save your local preferences on your device.
- Host access (`<all_urls>`) – so the lightbox can work on any page you choose to use it on. The extension reads page content locally in your browser to render the lightbox; it never sends that content anywhere.

## Third parties

Expand does not use third-party analytics, advertising, tracking, or data-sharing services.

## Children's privacy

Because Expand does not collect any data, it does not knowingly collect data from children or anyone else.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will be published in the extension's repository with a new "Last updated" date.

## Contact

For questions about this privacy policy, please open an issue in the project's repository.
